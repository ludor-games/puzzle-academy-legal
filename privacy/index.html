Puzzle Academy

Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Felipe Mata Marcos, trading as Bamba Games ("Bamba Games", "we", "us"), located in Spain, processes information when you use a pre-release or test version of Puzzle Academy (the "Game").

Information processed

The current Android test build may process:

  • Device and installation identifiers: a device-derived identifier used as an internal player ID; Firebase installation, analytics, Crashlytics and session identifiers; and advertising or similar device identifiers used by advertising services where available.
  • Technical and network information: device model and capabilities, operating system and app version, language, screen characteristics, IP address, network type or carrier, and diagnostic metadata.
  • Game activity: sessions, level attempts and outcomes, progression, rewards, feature interactions, timestamps, ad requests and ad interactions.
  • Crash and diagnostic information: stack traces, crash state, logs or breadcrumbs, memory and storage information, and related technical data needed to diagnose faults.
  • Purchase information: product, price, transaction and purchase-status metadata needed to offer and fulfil in-app purchases. Google Play processes payment details; Bamba Games does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Communications: information you choose to send when contacting support.

The Game does not ask you to create an account. The audited build does not request precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, audio recordings, or personal files. It schedules gameplay reminders locally if you grant notification permission; the audited game code does not register an account or device for remote promotional push messages.

Why we process information

We use information to operate and test the Game, save and understand progression, measure gameplay and test quality, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, provide advertising and rewarded-ad features, process purchases, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

For users in the European Economic Area, our legal bases are performance of the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Game, compliance with law, and consent where consent is required for advertising or similar storage/access technologies. You may withdraw consent through applicable device, platform, or consent controls without affecting processing already performed.

Advertising

The current configuration enables rewarded advertising through Appodeal. Interstitial ads are disabled in the audited configuration. Appodeal and its enabled mediation components—BidMachine, Bidon and associated technical analytics provided through Sentry—may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device/network information, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to request, deliver, measure, secure, and limit ads. Depending on your region, permissions, and consent choices, advertising partners may use information for personalized advertising.

The Game does not pass a name, email address, precise location, or its internal player ID to Appodeal through game code identified in the audit. Advertising integrations and demand partners can change in a later release; this Policy will be updated before materially different processing is introduced.

Service providers and sharing

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate features on our behalf, with authorities when legally required, or in connection with a legitimate transfer of the Game. Current providers include:

  • Google Firebase — Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Appodeal — rewarded-ad mediation and measurement.
  • BidMachine and Bidon — advertising auction/mediation components enabled through Appodeal.
  • Sentry — technical analytics included with the advertising integration.
  • Google Play — app distribution, purchases, and in-app review.

These providers may process data in countries outside Spain or the European Economic Area under their own terms and legally recognized transfer safeguards.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed for testing, operations, security, legal obligations, and the purposes above. Retention differs by provider. For example, Firebase states that Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers are generally retained for 90 days before deletion begins. Local game data remains on your device until removed by the Game, operating system, or uninstall process.

We and our providers use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may reset or restrict the Android advertising identifier, control ad personalization in your Google settings, deny notification permission, or uninstall the Game. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data, and to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your local supervisory authority.

To make a privacy request, email bambagamesbcn@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant test data. Some identifiers are device-generated and may not be reasonably linkable to your email without information from the device.

Children

This pre-release test is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum digital-consent age in their country. Children should not participate in testing or send personal information to us without authorization from a parent or guardian. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes

We may update this Policy as the test and its integrations change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate release or in-game notice where required.

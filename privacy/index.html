Puzzle Academy

Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Felipe Mata Marcos, trading as Bamba Games ("Bamba Games", "we", "us"), located in Spain, processes information when you use a pre-release or test version of Puzzle Academy (the "Game").

Information processed

The current Android and iOS test builds may process:

  • Device and installation identifiers: a device-derived identifier used as an internal player ID; Firebase installation, analytics, Crashlytics and session identifiers; and advertising or similar device identifiers used by advertising services where available.
  • Technical and network information: device model and capabilities, operating system and app version, language, screen characteristics, IP address, network type or carrier, and diagnostic metadata.
  • Game activity: sessions, level attempts and outcomes, progression, rewards, feature interactions, timestamps, ad requests and ad interactions.
  • Crash and diagnostic information: stack traces, crash state, logs or breadcrumbs, memory and storage information, and related technical data needed to diagnose faults.
  • Purchase information: product, price, transaction and purchase-status metadata needed to offer and fulfil in-app purchases. Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS) processes payment details; Bamba Games does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Communications: information you choose to send when contacting support.

The Game does not ask you to create an account. The audited build does not request precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, audio recordings, or personal files. It schedules gameplay reminders locally if you grant notification permission; the audited game code does not register an account or device for remote promotional push messages.

Why we process information

We use information to operate and test the Game, save and understand progression, measure gameplay and test quality, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, provide advertising and rewarded-ad features, process purchases, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

For users in the European Economic Area, our legal bases are performance of the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the Game, compliance with law, and consent where consent is required for advertising or similar storage/access technologies. You may withdraw consent through applicable device, platform, or consent controls without affecting processing already performed.

Advertising

The Game offers optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Interstitial ads are disabled in the current configuration. Ads are served through AppLovin MAX, which runs an auction among advertising networks; Google AdMob is currently the main network. AppLovin, Google and their demand partners may process advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA where you allow tracking), IP address, device and network information, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to request, deliver, measure, secure and limit ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your advertising consent the first time it starts, through Google's consent form. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalized. On iOS, the Game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared. You can change your tracking choice at any time in your iOS settings, and your advertising consent through the privacy option in the Game's Settings.

We use AppsFlyer to measure which advertising campaign led to an install, and to report the revenue that ads earn. AppsFlyer may process advertising identifiers where available, install referral data, IP address, device and app information, and ad revenue data. It starts only after the consent step above has run, and it respects the choices made there.

The Game does not pass a name, email address, precise location, or its internal player ID to AppLovin, Google or AppsFlyer through game code identified in the audit. Advertising integrations and demand partners can change in a later release; this Policy will be updated before materially different processing is introduced.

Service providers and sharing

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate features on our behalf, with authorities when legally required, or in connection with a legitimate transfer of the Game. Current providers include:

  • Google Firebase — Analytics (including ad revenue reporting) and Crashlytics.
  • AppLovin — rewarded-ad mediation (MAX), measurement, and its own advertising demand.
  • Google AdMob — advertising network, and Google's consent form in regions where consent is required.
  • AppsFlyer — install attribution and ad revenue measurement.
  • Google Play — Android app distribution, purchases, and in-app review.
  • Apple App Store — iOS app distribution and purchases.

These providers may process data in countries outside Spain or the European Economic Area under their own terms and legally recognized transfer safeguards.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed for testing, operations, security, legal obligations, and the purposes above. Retention differs by provider. For example, Firebase states that Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers are generally retained for 90 days before deletion begins. Local game data remains on your device until removed by the Game, operating system, or uninstall process.

We and our providers use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

On Android, you may reset or delete the advertising identifier and control ad personalization in your Google settings. On iOS, you may allow or deny tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On either platform, you may deny notification permission or uninstall the Game. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data, and to complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your local supervisory authority.

To make a privacy request, email bambagamesbcn@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant test data. Some identifiers are device-generated and may not be reasonably linkable to your email without information from the device.

Children

This pre-release test is intended for users aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at bambagamesbcn@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this Policy as the test and its integrations change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate release or in-game notice where required.
